Artistic Process

My Artistic Evolution

My creative process begins at the raw physical level. When constructing woodcraft pieces, I hand-select local mahogany blocks or custom plywood panels, treating and preparing them to avoid warping over time. Every pattern is drawn out freehand with fine graphite layout structures before paint, carving or carving tools touch the surface.

For mixed media work, such as textile engineering and ceramic work, I adapt local Sri Lankan heritage patterns using a modern layout methodology. Each piece requires meticulous layering, relying on multi-tier lacquer finishes or precise architectural plaster mixtures to achieve accurate physical relief depth.

Inside the Studio

Materials & Professional Tools

🛠️ 01. Sketching & Shaping Tools

Pencils & Erasers: Used for sketching the initial layout and guidelines directly onto the wooden surface.

Geometry Set: Compasses, rulers, and protractors used to measure precise circular shapes and maintain perfectly equal spacing.

Dotting Tools: Double-ended metal-tipped tools in various sizes, essential for creating uniform dots and details.

Color Palettes: Painting palettes used to mix and blend shades to create custom color combinations.

🖌️ 02. Brushes Collection

Background Brushes (Flat & Large Brushes): Wide, flat brushes used to apply the white primary base coat evenly across the Mahogany plate.

Fine Detail Brushes (00, 000, 0000): The thinnest, high-precision brush set, used to paint incredibly intricate designs completely freehand.

🎨 03. Paints & Mediums

Acrylic Paints: High-quality acrylic paint tubes and bottles from various premium brands, used to give the artwork its vibrant, lifelike colors (specifically yellows, blues, blacks, and whites).

White Gesso / Primer: A thick, white base coat applied to prime the raw wood surface, helping it absorb the paint smoothly and making the colors pop brilliantly.

Spray Varnish / Clear Sealer: A protective clear spray applied at the end to seal the finished painting, shielding it from dust, moisture, and scratches while ensuring a long-lasting finish.
